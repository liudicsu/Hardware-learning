## Pre-declared cells

All held and are disclosed in the en/zh results. Arm C’s lose mode was pre-declared as **wrong ICES only** (not an or-list, and not a torn or reversed 16-bit read).

- sparse_edges: paired A-B `period_ticks` and `first_ticks` both `0.0000+-0.0000: tie: identical on every seed` (`800.0000` tick period, `200.0000` first stamp; 手算 `400.0000 us`, `2500.0000 Hz`). Declared exact tie or official near-tie; held as an exact tie; kept.
- short_pulses: paired A-B `n_capt` `-16.0000+-0.0000`, **clear**, same sign on all 5 seeds (A captures `0.0000`; B captures all `16.0000` rising edges). B clearly better; held; kept.
- icr_overwrite: official late-read ICR `108.0000+-0.0000` ticks (second edge overwrites); keep-first `100.0000+-0.0000` disclosed and kept; A’s first stamp `100.0000+-0.0000`. Paired B-keep_first `ICR_ticks` `8.0000+-0.0000`, **small but consistent** (8 ticks is under 20 % of the smaller-arm mean 100), same sign on all 5 seeds. Official overwrite held; kept.
- wrong_ices: paired C-B `first_ticks` `40.0000+-0.0000`, **clear**, same sign on all 5 seeds (C first stamp `240.0000` vs B `200.0000`; 手算 `120.0000 us` vs `100.0000 us`). C’s falling-to-falling period still matches the official rising period when pulse width is constant; C loses on the first-capture stamp. Held; kept.
- Named alternative `POLL_PERIOD_ALT_TICKS=1` was printed on the short-pulse schedule and not adopted (paired A-B `n_capt` `0.0000+-0.0000: tie: identical on every seed`).
