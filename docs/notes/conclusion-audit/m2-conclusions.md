Sections in `src/content/m2/*/en.mdx` (zh.mdx verified to carry the same content) that state a conclusion or a number without the hardware background or intermediate steps a beginner needs. Sections that derive their numbers (e.g. ADC→volts, RC filter, millis() overflow, LEDC duty) or give only instructions are not listed.
