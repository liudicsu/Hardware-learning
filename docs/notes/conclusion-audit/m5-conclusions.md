# Month 5 — conclusions and numbers stated without their derivation

Scope: every `src/content/m5/*/en.mdx` was read in full (control-modelling, state-space-lqr, kinematics-ik,
camera-calibration, moveit-pick-place). Each row below is a `##`/`###` section that states a conclusion, a number
or a table of results without giving the reader the formula, the number's source, or the mechanism needed to
follow it. Sections that already derive their numbers (control-modelling "Why model the joint" and "Identify the
model from data", kinematics-ik "Forward kinematics" and the det J row of "Singularities", camera-calibration
"The pinhole model" and "From a pixel to 3D") are **not** listed, nor are sections that only give instructions.
Every gap listed here exists identically in the matching `zh.mdx` of the same lesson (verified: same headings, same
figures), so each fix must be applied to both files.

| src/content/m5/control-modelling/en.mdx | `## From margins to step response` | Prediction asserted with no mechanism: "The same margin predicts that the loop should ring at the phase crossover, 40.2 rad/s, and turn unstable near kp = 6.79" — nothing says why a margin implies a ringing frequency or a critical gain, and neither 40.2 rad/s nor 6.79 is derived in the prose (both only appear in the script output). | Add the sustained-oscillation condition `\|L\| = 1` with `∠L = −180°`: scaling kp changes magnitude but not phase, so −180° can only be met at ω_pc (hence the 40.2 rad/s ringing) and the gain there must rise by `10^(16.6/20) = 6.8`, giving `kp_crit = 1.0 × 6.8 = 6.8 ≈ 6.79`. |
