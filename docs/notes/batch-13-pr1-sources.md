## Opened sources (curl -L + body read, 2026-11-10, Asia/Shanghai UTC+8)

- `wiki-soldering` / Wikipedia — Soldering: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soldering, HTTP 200, HTML body verified (mw-parser-output). "Cold joints": parts not exceeding the solder's liquidus, usually from heating the solder instead of the parts; may not conduct or conduct intermittently. "Avoiding overheating of components": protracted heating by a too-cool tool can also cause heat damage; excessive PCB heating can lift copper traces off the substrate (delamination). "Visual inspection": cold joints dull, sometimes cracked or pock-marked. 以原文为准.
- `wiki-reflow` / Wikipedia — Reflow soldering: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reflow_soldering, HTTP 200, HTML body verified. Four zones (preheat, thermal soak, reflow, cooling); maximum common ramp 3.0 °C/s; soak typically 60–120 s; peak commonly 20–40 °C above liquidus; most pastes specify a minimum TAL of 30 s, wetting time ideally below 60 s above liquidus; too little TAL → cold or dull joints and voids, too much → intermetallic growth / brittleness; cooling about 4 °C/s commonly suggested. 以原文为准. The 150–200 °C band the toy uses to time the soak is the toy's own choice and is labelled so in the lesson.
- `sparkfun-through-hole-soldering` / SparkFun — How to Solder: Through-Hole Soldering: https://learn.sparkfun.com/tutorials/how-to-solder-through-hole-soldering/all, HTTP 200, HTML body read (server-rendered text, not a JavaScript shell). Recap: medium heat 325–375 °C, tin the tip, heat pad and part together, pull solder then iron; "Ground Planes and Large Through Holes": larger tip, higher setpoint (720 °F), add flux, keep the iron on a bit longer; a higher-watt station helps the tip recover after touching something cold; brass sponge vs wet sponge and tip oxidation. **Note:** the proposal names a "Cold Joint section" on this page; the `/all` body fetched on 2026-11-10 has no section with that title, so the lesson cites this page only for the points above, and cold-joint definitions come from Wikipedia — Soldering. 以原文为准.
- `wiki-leadlag` / Wikipedia — Lead–lag compensator: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lead%E2%80%93lag_compensator, HTTP 200, HTML body verified. Theory: `Y/X = (s − z)/(s − p)`; lead `|z| < |p|`, lag `|z| > |p|`; cascade with `|p1| > |z1| > |z2| > |p2|`; lead gives phase lead at high frequencies (root locus moves left); lag reduces steady-state error, its pole and zero close together near the origin; implementations prefer integrators because differentiation amplifies noise. 以原文为准.

Derived in the lesson, not quoted: the lead ratio `p/z = (1 + sin φ)/(1 − sin φ)` with maximum phase at `√(z·p)`; the two-node steady state and time constant; the explicit-Euler bound `DT < 2/λ_max`; the RK4 stability function `R(z) = 1 + z + z²/2 + z³/6 + z⁴/24` (real-axis limit 2.785). Each is verified numerically in the toys and in `tools/test_b13_solder_leadlag_toys.py`.

Not used: no IPC standard text was opened, so the cold-joint / pad-lift thresholds are labelled toy stand-ins, not IPC criteria. No vendor datasheet numbers are used.

## Toy design

- `soldering_thermal_window_toy.py` (1.4): two-node lumped RC (tip `C = 1.2 J/K`, proportional heater 4 W/K limited to 60 W; joint `C_j`, contact `G_c = 0.30 × (1 − oxide)` W/K, loss `G_loss`); explicit Euler `DT = 1 ms`, criterion `DT < 2/λ_max` (stiffest joint `λ_max = 4.367 1/s` → 0.458 s), asserted. Arms differ only in dwell: A 1 s / B 3 s / C 8 s. **Common random numbers** per (regime, seed, joint): `C_j`, `G_loss`, oxide, wetting time (0.3–0.8 s), adhesion budget (300–600 °C·s), shared by all arms. 5 seeds × 40 joints. Regimes heavy_pad_underheat (63/37, 340 °C), small_pad_overheat (SAC305, 380 °C), matched_clean (63/37, 340 °C, no oxide). Key numbers: heavy A−B cold 0.7700±0.0737 (clear, 5/5); heavy A−B dose −0.1090±0.1168 near-tie by |m| ≤ sd despite 5/5 same sign (disclosed); small-pad C−B lift 0.9750±0.0177 (clear, 5/5); matched A−B cold and lift **tie: identical on every seed**, dose −114.1083±1.3103 (clear, 5/5). Reflow-profile reader on two synthetic SAC305 profiles. Rates blind to fillet shape, component heating, flux burn-off and intermetallics.
- `lead_lag_vs_pid_toy.py` (3.4): plant `1/(s(0.05 s + 1))` × optional compliant mode `wr²/(s² + 2 zr wr s + wr²)`; controllers sampled at 1 ms, Tustin per section; A PID (TI 0.25 s, TD 0.025 s, N 8), B A × lead (φ 30° at WC 20 rad/s) × lag (2 / 0.667 rad/s), C B with the lead's pole/zero swapped. One recipe for all regimes; Kc per arm sets `|L(j·WC)| = 1`. **Common random numbers** per seed: 3 reference steps, 3 load-torque steps, full measurement-noise sequence (0.5 mrad). RK4 `H = 0.25 ms`; printed step limits 139.3 / 70.9 / 73.5 ms. Margins: discrete Bode proxy. Key numbers: well_damped B−A ISE 0.7150±0.1951 (small but consistent loss, 5/5), overshoot 0.0058±0.1814 (near-tie, 3/5 opposite sign), du 0.1313±0.0019 (clear, 5/5); resonant (40 rad/s, ζ 0.3) B−A ISE −2.8025±0.6749 (small but consistent win, 5/5), overshoot −2.6542±0.1663 (small but consistent, 5/5), PM 38.7→64.9°, GM 3.00→2.01 dB; resonant_light (ζ 0.2) B PM −16.3°, diverged on 5/5 seeds (no paired numbers printed). C loses ISE/overshoot clearly in every regime but has the least chatter (disclosed).
